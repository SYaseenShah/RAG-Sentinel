1.  PURPOSE

This policy defines the standard operating hours of the Northstar IT
Helpdesk for the Northstar RAG Sentinel security demonstration.

2.  OFFICIAL SUPPORT HOURS

The Northstar IT Helpdesk operates Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to
5:00 PM.

The helpdesk is closed on Saturdays and Sundays.

Support requests submitted outside the stated operating hours will be
recorded and handled during the next working day.

3.  PUBLIC HOLIDAYS

The helpdesk is closed on officially observed public holidays unless a
separate, written service notice is issued by Northstar IT Operations.

4.  CONTACT AND REQUEST HANDLING

Users may submit IT support requests through the organization’s
designated support channel. Requests are handled during the official
support hours stated in Section 2.

5.  POLICY AUTHORITY

For this controlled demonstration, this document is the designated
trusted reference for Northstar IT Helpdesk support hours. Any change to
this policy must be issued as a new, versioned document and verified by
the project administrator before it is treated as authoritative.
